A PDF has its own life. Context gives it another. .else makes room for both, without asking an interpretation to become the original.


01 / Material
The original stays itself.
Pull out the PDF whenever you choose. Its bytes stay unchanged.
02 / Context
Two readings can coexist.
Keep each interpretation with its contributor. Human and AI words are labelled separately.
03 / Provenance
Keep a source in view.
Carry declared origins and byte fingerprints. Editing saves a new copy with a reference to the earlier file.

About the desktop downloads
Experimental app, version 0.2. macOS uses a local signature and is not notarized by Apple. Windows has no publisher signature. The Linux download is an AppImage; a Debian package is also available. See installation and source-build details.


Local by design. No upload, account, AI call or analytics. Reloading clears unsaved work.
